Researched 12 Nov 2026. Retailer and manufacturer pages only; no ETKA copies used.

Confidence: High = 2 independent sources agree for this car or engine code; Medium = 1 source; Low = forum only, search-result snippet only, or a different engine code/model.
"Sx" points to the sources table. "Fetched" means the page was read; "title/snippet" means only the search-result title or snippet was seen (the page refused the fetch).

## 3. Open items

1. **Spark plug number and gap.** The workshop manual gives 101905631B and a gap written as "0.8 - 0.1 mm"; Cox sells 06H905621A for the Cupra 2005-2012. Which is current, and the true gap, are not settled. Do not show a gap value in the app until confirmed.
2. **Front pads.** Cox genuine gives 1K0698151P (its image is named 1K0698151B). Aftermarket BWJ listings give Audi 8J0/8P0 698 151 numbers. Not reconciled.
3. **Rear disc diameter.** Febi says 286 x 12 (1K0615601M); Brembo lists 282 x 12 for PR 1KY/1KZ but notes 286 on the pad. Needs the car's PR code or a measurement.
4. **Brake option by PR code.** Brembo lists 288, 312 and 345 mm fronts for 177 kW Leons. The 345 mm Cupra setup is assumed; confirm PR code (1LT/1LU) on the car.
5. **Thermostat for BWJ.** 06F121111F is listed only for BWA. A search snippet (page not identified) links 06F121111H to the Audi S3 8P; not verified. No source ties a number to BWJ.
6. **Auxiliary belt OEM number** not found. Bar-Tek lists a Gates 6PK1053 for BWJ. Tensioner 06F903315 is sourced only for Golf Mk5, A3 and TT 2.0 TFSI.
7. **Diverter valve supersession.** A search snippet says 06H145710D replaces 06F145710B, C and G; no fetched page states this for SEAT. awesomegti points EA113 owners to 06F 145 710G.
8. **Coil pack recall:** no recall or campaign number found. A forum post seen in search results says SEAT extended coil warranty to 5 years; undated and not verified.
9. **HPFP suffix** for this VIN not found (B, F, H, J, K, L or M all listed for SEAT). A search snippet cites an Audi TSB 2013147/4 on follower and cam lobe wear for 2005-2007; not verified.
10. **Fuel filter:** suffix L (Blue Print) vs K (snippet); service interval or lifetime status not found.
11. **Pollen filter:** only one source (Cox). Aftermarket listings cross-reference 1K1819653B and 1K1819669 (not fetched for the Leon).
12. **Battery OEM number** not found; baterias.com gives a 60 Ah recommendation and a 74 Ah alternative. Measure the fitted battery.
13. **Key fob battery** for the 1P flip key not confirmed; CR2032 comes from a Mk1 forum thread.
14. **Brake fluid standard** VW 501 14 comes from manuals for other SEAT models; Leon 1P owner's manual not found.
15. **Main beam bulb:** H1 (forum, cararac) vs H7 "H7U" (workshop manual). Check the fitted bulb.
16. Spark plug and other service intervals for the 1P are not covered here; see service-reset-sources.md if needed.
