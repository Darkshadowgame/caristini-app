# SEAT Leon Cupra 1P (BWJ, 02Q) owner's reference: sourced facts

Research note, 12 Nov 2026. Car: 2007 SEAT Leon Cupra, type 1P1, VIN VSSZZZ1PZ7R000000, 2.0 TFSI BWJ (EA113, 177 kW / 240 PS, Bosch MED 9.1), 6-speed manual 02Q (KDU or KPA), UK car.

Confidence: High = 2 independent sources agree. Medium = 1 source. Low = forum post only, or a source for another engine or model. Only High and Medium items go into `cupra-reference.json`. Source IDs (S1, S2 ...) point to the sources table.

Main sources: the SEAT Leon owner's manual for this generation (S1, part 1P0012003AE, edition 07.06, covers the 2.0 177 kW / 240 bhp engine) and the SEAT factory workshop manual pages for the 1P hosted on workshop-manuals.com (S2 to S12; pages name engine codes BWA and BWJ and gearbox 02Q). Ross-Tech label files were not used.

## Open items

1. Engine oil capacity: owner's manual 5.3 l versus workshop manual 4.6 l with filter. Measure at the next oil change, or check the car's own handbook supplement.
2. Coolant: owner's manual says G12+ (TL-VW 774 F); workshop manual says BWJ must use G12++ (TL VW 774 G). G12++ meets both. Capacity not found for the Leon.
3. Tyre pressures for 225/40 R18 on the Cupra: only a generic 1P table (2.4/2.2 bar). Read the label in the fuel flap and record it in the app.
4. Wheel offset of the actual Cupra wheel: ET51 or ET53 listed; not confirmed. Read the stamp on the wheel.
5. Timing belt: Haynes says 180,000 km with no age limit; dealers and forums say 4 years. No SEAT document seen with an age limit. Ask a SEAT dealer with the VIN.
6. LongLife interval for a 2007 UK car: not found in an official source. Check the service book for which schedule (fixed or LongLife) the car is on.
7. Battery Ah and CCA: not found. Read the fitted battery label.
8. 02Q oil spec: SEAT points to the parts catalogue; G 052 171 A2 is from an Audi A3 chart. Confirm with a parts lookup on the VIN.
9. Recalls: DVSA database could not be read. Run the VIN through the GOV.UK recall checker by hand and ask SEAT UK. The ABS/ESP earth recall covers builds from 12 Nov 2007; this car's build date is not known.
10. Faults other than the cam follower rest on US 2.0T FSI (BPY) sources, retailers or forums. VAG 5-digit codes for these faults were not found, except 17963 on a different engine.
11. Lamp failure as a known 1P fault: not found.
